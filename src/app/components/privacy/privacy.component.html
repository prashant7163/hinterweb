Privacy Policy

Welcome to Hinter!

In this privacy policy, we sometimes refer to “You”. “ You” may be a visitor or a user of one or more of our platforms. This policy does not apply to third-party websites, products, or services even if they link to our Services, and You should consider the privacy practices of those third-parties carefully.

1. Types of information Hinter is collecting

  • The information you provide

    Hinter collects the content and other information you hand over while using the application. This includes signing up to Hinter, creating or sharing, and messaging or communicating with other Users. The information you provides, except of content, comprises also of location, photos or the date a file was created. Hinter also collects the information about how you are using our application, for example the types of content you search, view and engage with or the frequency and duration of your activities.

  • Your networks and connections

    Hinter collects information about the Users you are connected to and how do you interact with them. We also collect contact information you provide if you import or sync this type of data (e.g. an address book) from a device.

  • Device information

    Hinter collects information from and about the devices where you install or access our application. That depends on the permissions you have granted. This information can include for example:

    • Version of hardware and device settings, operating system details, names and types of software, battery and signal strength of battery and signal.
    • Device location through GPS, Bluetooth, or WiFi.
    • Connection information: mobile operator or ISP, browser type, language, time zone, phone number and IP address.

2. How does Hinter use this information

Hinter uses all of the information collected to improve the performance of the application, develop new features and provide you support. We also use the information about you reply to you when you contact us. Collected from the Users information, helps us to verify accounts and activity, and to assure safety and security of our application.

3. How the information you provide is shared

As per current version of Hinter, all content you creates within the application (e.g new tapes, milestones, targets as well as your interaction over others Users content) is public. Selection of the audience with who you want to share your content will be introduced in the new versions of Hinter.

The public information is available to anyone who is also registered in Hinter or can be seen or accessed through online search engines and APIs.

Information provided by you is not shared with any third party organisations.

Hinter may share your information only in response to a legal request, for example from jurisdictions.

4. How long your data is stored

Hinter stores data for as long as it is needed to provide you and others our services. Information associated with your account will be kept until your account is deleted, unless Hinter no longer need this information.

You can delete your profile any time. Once you delete your account, Hinter will delete all of the things you have created within the application.

5. How will Hinter notify you of the updates of this policy

Hinter will notify you beforehand applying changes to this policy and give you the chance to review the changed policy before continuing to use the app.
