Extensive experience search tool where you can search any action, big or super small.
Every single search result is an experience of other user (and this experience can be seen in a context of a whole life).
Create your life biography in an easy and convenient way by entering your life happenings from past, present or planned for the future.
These happenings are placed in a form of cards on an auto-generated LifeMap, which is unique for every user – see how your LifeMap turns out to be.
Create group experiences with your Buddies
where everyone can share their individulal experiences in a collective manner.
Hey, How about bad experiences? They also can become hints to a better Life!
Combine your precious privacy with the access of valuable information. Share your experiences anonymously.
Manage experiences by their own threads.
Segregate your experiences according to different dimensions of your Life: career, health, hobbies, relationships et cetera.
